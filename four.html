Celebrating 33 Years of Jurassic Movies

This page celebrates 33 years of Jurassic films and their impact on how people imagine dinosaurs. It highlights iconic moments, memorable designs, and how dinosaurs in film have evolved over time. While inspired by entertainment, it also connects these portrayals to real science, showing how our understanding of dinosaurs has changed alongside the movies.
